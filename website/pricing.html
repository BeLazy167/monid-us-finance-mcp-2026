Pricing & honest scope

Financial Datasets bills a subscription plus per-request credits. We charge nothing up front and take no cut — you bring your own Monid API key (get one at monid.ai), every call runs on that key, and you pay only the measured per-call Monid cost, straight to your own Monid wallet. We never store your key.

Measured across the 51 priced calls in the committed ledger: total $0.0515, mean $0.00101, median $0.0006. 82% of calls cost $0.0006, 14% cost $0.0009, and 4% cost $0.0100. At the mean we are roughly half Financial Datasets’ $0.002 included rate and about 90% below its $0.010 Build overage rate. The honest caveat: our cost is per-route and variable, not flat. A workload made entirely of the $0.0100 routes costs 5× the included rate — about $1,000 for 100,000 calls against $200 on Build. High-volume, extraction-heavy usage is cheaper on a subscription.

What we do NOT have

  • No real-time era. No tick websockets or sub-second pricing streams; prices are daily/weekly/monthly bars and point-in-time snapshots.
  • No 30-year normalized depth. Sourced from DefiLlama US-equities beta and SEC EDGAR; we do not claim a proprietary 30-year normalized history.
  • No SLAs, no redistribution, no webhooks. Distributed multi-provider routing without enterprise service level agreements. The paid tiers we compare against also include data redistribution rights, webhooks, zero data retention and bulk delivery; we offer none of those.
  • Partial tools. earnings (10-K/10-Q only, no estimates), insider trades (capped 15-row feed), screener (exchange + market_cap, eq only), financial metrics (valuation fields omitted), all 27 MCP tools are implemented, while 2 REST routes answer a zero-cost not_implemented stub and 8 Financial Datasets routes are not registered at all.
  • Omitted over fabricated. Where the source cannot honestly provide a field, it is omitted — never guessed.
Prices are measured on live Monid calls from docs/live-smoke.md and the receipts ledger; they can vary with Monid rates. Always treat per-call cost as the authoritative number from your own receipt.
