They are faster. We took out the toll booth.

Financial Datasets reads a database they own, and on a first ask they are about twenty times quicker than we are. We compose the answer from live providers, then hold it for five minutes, and inside that window we are the faster one. Both facts are here, at the same size.

What it costs to ask

Their cheapest per-request rate is real and it beats ours. It also arrives welded to a subscription. We have no floor: you host the binary and pay the providers for the calls you make.

Is that many calls even reachable?

Yes, and it is worth knowing which side of the line you are on before you pick. There are 8,005 tickers in the SEC ticker file this server indexes. Four datasets for every one of them, once a month, is already past the crossover.
