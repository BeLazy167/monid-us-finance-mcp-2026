Speed, honestly

They are faster. We are cheaper to keep asking.

Financial Datasets serves from a database they own. We compose an answer from live providers on first ask, which is slower by an order of magnitude, and then serve it from a five-minute cache, which is faster than they are. Both numbers are below. Neither is hidden.

If your workload needs sub-200 ms reads on cold data, buy theirs. That is not a hedge, it is the measurement.
